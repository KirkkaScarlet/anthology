## Introduction

A [[Dragonkin]] who found herself loathing her own kind, Alexis set out on adventure in her early years seeking her calling. Her bright and outgoing attitude made her many allies along the way, though her trust in others has been betrayed more than once. Trained as a merchant by her mentor, she fell in love with travel and trade and worked until becoming the Guildmaster of the Merchants' Guild, overseeing all trade in the region.

## Appearance

**Human Form**: Alex, as she's called in her human form, appears as a very short human girl. Her slender frame would conceal her draconic background well, was it not for the amethyst eyes and matching hair betraying it as they shimmer slightly in the light.

**Dragon Form:** Alexis’ dragon form is nothing unusual for a Dragonkin. Her bloodline's amethyst scales coat her body as a large pair of wings wrap close around her and her tail swishes passively behind her. Small black horns protrude from the sides of her head and then upward. The left horn is cracked, with cloth wrapping around, trying its hardest to keep things together. Her form conveys immense strength at first glance, though Alexis is quick to reveal she's honed her speed far more than her strength. 

Regardless of the form she is in, a sword rests sheathed against Alexis' hip, with her hand on its pommel. She is well dressed, exposing her position as someone with a lot of wealth. Though her clothes look quite refined, Alexis has no trouble moving around in them in any situation. Her clothes shift with her form automatically, unfolding and opening at hidden seams when transforming into her larger form, or the opposite into her smaller human form. A common enchantment for Dragonkin clothing.

## Personality

As any leader should be, Alexis is a charismatic, well-educated Dragonkin. She is always friendly toward others and is always excited to lead conversations off track with eccentric stories. While her stories tend to be exaggerated, honesty is her number one concern whenever a serious topic comes up. That said, there is a constant hint of calculation and planning behind every word she says, making it difficult to truly grasp the angle she is working, if she is working one at all.

At the negotiation table, a high level of confidence comes from the woman, gained from many years of success as she rose up the ranks of the Merchants' Guild. Her persuasive abilities stem from her unique personality and lively conversations. Her ambition of strengthening the Merchants' Guild position across the land drives her forward.

Alexis treats everyone who can pay equally. Her upbringing brought prejudice against her and her chromatic caste, fostering a strong dislike of her own people.  As she grew and came to learn the value of money, she understood such hatred even less. Different species mean little in the face of Gold, after all.

Like many of her kin, she worships Uriel, the God of Light, but does not make a big deal about it much of the time. She considers her religious thoughts separate from any of her guild duties, and rarely speaks of it.

These days, Alexis focuses her long list of resources on strengthening her Guild. She knows it requires an open mind, something she has worked to develop for a long time, with lots of patience. Various factions all have their differences, and she may or may not agree with them, but they all have one thing in common. They all use Gold, and that means they are all potential friends.

## History

Alexis was born to Eleanor and Nathaniel Holt, a family of Dragonkin in Indgarde, the capital city of the Utgardian Theocracy, just over 190 years ago. Sharing her parents' chromatic caste, her family lived a challenging life as targets of prejudice, but they managed to get through the days. As Alexis grew and learned about her people, however, it became apparent that she could never be happy living among them. Her people's attitude toward her caste would limit her potential. She desired more for herself, and being trapped near the bottom of the food chain was not something she looked forward to for the next few hundred years.

She sought an opportunity to leave her homeland and explore. And she met that opportunity, a traveling merchant by the name of Henry Knowles. Henry often visited Indgarde and knew Alexis’ father through his trade. Whenever Henry visited, Alexis spent all her time trying to learn as much as she could about the world outside Utgardia. It was from Henry that she learned about the Merchants' Guild that he was a member of, as well as how it operated. Enamored with the idea of traveling, but against her parents wishes, she left Indgarde after she came of age, traveling with Henry and learning trade as Henry’s apprentice. By Henry's recommendation, she spent much of her time in her human form as Dragonkin were exceedingly rare outside of Utgardia. Although, she eventually got used to acting as Henry's bodyguard, considering her draconic form provided her with ample physical prowess, not to mention the Dragonkin's innate magical powers. The pair grew into close friends as mentor and student, as well as merchant and bodyguard, until an illness claimed Henry’s life after nearly twenty years of friendship.

Having seen what she had in her travels, Alexis, who now went by Alex thanks to Henry’s penchant to shorten everyone’s name except his own, headed to the city of Cynd to officially join the Merchants' Guild as a full merchant instead of an apprentice. She had quickly come to love all that being a merchant brought: exploration, meeting new people, and making lots of Gold... the last one being the most valuable. So Alex says when asked, at least.

Despite having a good amount of experience under her belt, Alex quickly came to understand that being a merchant without Henry's connections and the groundwork he had laid over the years was a challenge. She struggled for several years, before being forced to rely harder on her bodyguard skills to make ends meet while still allowing her to get most of her desired experiences. 

She continued on that path for around ten years, and as she did she experience all the different sides of humanity. Under-the-table deals, cheating, scams, and swindling were more common than she realized, and she was quickly becoming tired as someone being hired not to protect, but to intimidate and threaten. The Guild itself was no better. It struggled to police its own members at times, likely due to corruption and greed.

Deciding to take a break, she returned home to Utgardia. She'd not seen her family in some years, but when she arrived she was disheartened to see that things had only worsened there. Her parents still lived in the same house, but their land had been claimed by a powerful family of red dragons, members of the wealthiest caste. They were practically slaves at that point, but her parents couldn't see past their own education, believing that this is how things are supposed to be. The will of Uriel, the God of Light, they say. 

Alex stayed for only a short time, leaving after failing to convince her parents to come with her. She said her goodbye and promised to write, despite their opposition to her leaving a second time. The reality was that her homeland was trapped in their religious beliefs. Alex believed in her god as well, though the teaching left much to be desired.

Another handful of years passed and she found herself with a rare opportunity: Settle down and open a store. Alexander Ravnskov, a human from the city of Silma, had hired her for some bodyguard work and it did not take long for the two to fall in love. Alexis went back to her old name to minimize awkwardness, though they both found it amusing and would often use it to play tricks on others.

Three years of working together as traveling merchants, the pair opened a store selling goods brought in from throughout the Empire. They did modestly well, and stayed busy. However, an illness claimed Alexander's life around 20 years after they opened the store. He was getting up there in age, but Alexis thought it strange. She thought about Henry and the coincidence. Was she the cause, somehow?

Making another trip back to her home, she tried to visit her parents but they were nowhere to be found. Her home she grew up in lay abandoned, clearly empty for numerous years. She thought to try and track them, but quickly found herself exiled when a violent group attacked her on sight, and forced her to flee. She learned through quietly asking around that it was proven that chromatic dragons poison those around them, and they have been exiled... or purged. Not having the strength to fight against such a united people's who believed this, she said her goodbyes alone, praying she'd one day see her parents again. 

Now at the age of 92, Alexis wanted to separate herself from her life in Silma. She sold her storefront turned home, and began traveling, seeking knowledge about her supposed toxic nature. Humans were much more open minded, after all. She would not struggle to find those looking to help. As long as the Gold is right. 

She was unable to learn much as first; humans and dragonkin rarely interacted considering how rare it was for a dragonkin to leave their homeland. But she persevered, and eventually met Alister Agrew, a famous healer from the northlands. They spent around a year together as he studied her affliction and its effects. It was apparently some kind of side affect of her magic. She would emanate just the faintest bit of energy constantly, corrupting those around her. The effect was apparently very minor considering it took around twenty years of living side by side together to kill them. But there it was. Alister claimed he would search for a cure, but had none to offer at the time. Alexis would go on to visit Alister every few years after he convinced her he was able to shield himself from its effects. In the meantime, she spent much of her time away from others, fearful of the affliction somehow worsening.

In her time alone, she focused herself on study. She still loved her home in the Empire, and still wanted to be a merchant. But there were issues. Corruption was starting to get rampant and Alexis wanted to do something about it. But what could she do when she can't even be near people? Still, if something could be done, she wanted to do it. So she studied and gathered information, preparing herself for a future that may never come.

As nearly forty years passed since her discovery of herself, on what would be her last visit to Alister's home in the north, Alister proposed a possible solution. He taught Alexis a spell that would cause her magic to cease functioning, explaining that if she managed it well, she could still use her powers when she needed them by disabling the spell, then reactivating it when no longer needed. Grateful, Alexis left Alister and age claimed his life just two years later. She practiced her spellcasting and worked to master the Second Chance Alister gave her. As much as she could, she quieted the thoughts that Alister's death could be her fault as well. 

Alexis sought to return to the Merchants' Guild, and though so much time had passed and nearly everyone was a new face, Alexis found the same issues present that the Guild had in its past. Greed doesn't have to come at the expense of others... or so she believed. And she was ready to put it to the test. 

It took her around forty years, but she worked to rise through the ranks of the Merchants' Guild, all the way up to Guildmaster. She realized it could have gone much faster had she been a little more underhanded on things, but she wanted to change things the right way. Her lengthy lifespan served her well here, as it allowed her to accumulate enough experience beat those who were more talented, as well as simply outlive others. She'd long gained mastery over her own affliction by this point, to the point she could passively control it and that it was no longer an issue. Or at least, so the healers she inquired about said.

For seventeen years she worked as Guildmaster to bring large reforms to the guild, using her decades and in some cases, over a century of connections and knowledge to weaken the corruption within. And it was clear that it was working... but there was more to be done. 

Then, while returning from a business trip, Alex and Arthur Clausen--her young, upstart bodyguard--found themselves lost in an unexplained mist while on their way back to Cynd. 
They never emerged.
