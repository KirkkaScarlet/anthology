## Backstory

Rihne hails from the Saribas Isles, a small collection of islands a few hundred miles to the south of the Stoneheart Mountains of Auros. The islands are home to a group of Dan'Illayan descended humans numbering in the thousands. They have spent some time being isolated from the rest of the world, as not many have reason to visit except a small collection of merchants to trade.

Nearly all aspects of life in Saribas revolves around magic. Magic is studied very heavily by scholars, and standard education includes basic training for magic usage. Living on the isles can be difficult so the use of magic is extremely common in all things.

The religion of Saribas involves deep worship in the form of respect toward the arcane. The arcane magic that flows throughout the world represents all aspects of life, death, and everything in between. A large centerpiece of this religion are Mystics, a specialized group of scholars who dedicate their lives to the study of mana and the arcane, and lead communities in ritualistic ceremonies involving arcane enchanted dances. When they wear their Mystic garbs, often lined with mana infused jewelry, clothing, or trinkets, moving in specific patterns can manipulate and control the mana around them to create a wide variety of magical effects.

Rihne was born in T'koas, the capital city of Saribas. It was quickly discovered that she had a natural aptitude for magic, and was therefore assigned to the scholarly Kirye tribe at the Age of Assignment. Raised by the tribe, she became adept at magic and constantly craved more knowledge of the arcane and therefore, the world itself. As she grew up she became fairly well known for her aptitude but was always a little standoffish as she often preferred studying or practicing over socializing. That did not stop her from making friends, however, as her best friends C'eria Fayzn Nās and T’koas Nyame Kirye have been a major part of her life.

Recently, she began spending time near C'sera, the main port city of Saribas. Merchants from around the world occasionally visit and trade both supplies and information, including stories. As the stories began to turn toward a focus on The Plague, a major event that had plunged the continent of Auros into something of a dark time. Zombies roamed the lands and people were being subjected to mind control effects and the like.

Few were concerned about these tales, but Rihne wanted to learn more. Powerful chaos magic had to be the source of such a major event, and as a scholar of the arcane, studying all forms of magic was her life. She eventually convinced the Sariban leader, C'eria Tamir Ka'nan, to send an expedition to Auros to collect more information. Twelve members were sent, but Rihne was not one of them as her focus was not on Chaos magic, so she was not chosen.

Nearly six months passed before Tamir Ka'nan decided to send another group after the first never returned. Their mission was only to find their people then return. This group was also twelve members, except this time, eight were highly skilled warriors while the other four were Rihne, her friend Nyame, and two others from the Kirye tribe. Upon arriving, it only took the group a few weeks to discover the fate of their previous group. An unexpected attack on the city from necromancers led to their deaths. Whether they were the target of this attack or not remained a mystery, but there were only about 35 deaths in total. All twelve saribans, and then several city guards.

Once this was discovered, the group spent a few days searching for additional information, before deciding to return. All except Rihne, that is. She was convinced that there was more going on than they were being led to believe and wanted to stay. The others were not on board with this idea, and all of them left except Fayzn, who stayed behind with Rihne as her guard. After a few days, Rihne and Fayzn joined with a caravan on the way out to Ashford, a city further inland. However, while on the road, just after dusk, the caravan was attacked. Fayzn and several other caravan guards and fighters jumped into the fray to protect the others. However, the driver in charge of Rihne's caravan decided to flee instead of following orders and staying put. Rhine's wagon made it to Ashford safely, but Fayzn never arrived, nor did anyone else. When it was reported, a search party was sent out. After another day, the party returned saying they have some trackers following tracks heading toward Aldore, the elven lands.

Rihne took the day to rest and was planning on finding someone to take her there when the city was attacked by necromancers once again. Rihne joined with some fellow adventurers at the inn she was staying at and fought, before being conscripted into the Huntsmen in exchange for receiving assistance with finding Fayzn.

## Appearance

Rihne is of average height for her people, standing at 5’3”. She has lengthy blonde hair that is braided into two low ponytails, one of several traditional hairstyles from her homeland. Her skin is an olive tone seemingly under a permanent tan. She is considered fairly attractive in her homeland, and that likely remains true among the other varieties of humans.

  
The outfit she wears is a modified version of a traditional islander dancer garb. It has a variety of jewelry and other trinkets all about it that are enchanted to be able to control mana more easily, which is how Rihne directs and casts spells. Faint magical effects occasionally flicker and light from these, though this effect can be muted by the wearer at will.

Finally, she carries a traditional bo staff with her, a ceremonial staff enchanted with magic to strengthen it as a defensive tool, though Rihne has little training in that regard. She always utilizes the staff in her dances and other ritual magic, but it is not a requirement to cast spells for her.

## Personality

Rihne is fairly quiet but friendly when engaged. If left alone she'll keep busy by reading whatever she can get her hands on. If she can't do that, she'll seek out people who have information and learn whatever she can, often trading information when required. While she is heavily focused on studying, she never shies away from helping others in need and she does enjoy engaging with people, she just simply doesn't initiate.

Despite being such a bookworm, Rihne struggles to sit still. She will regularly get up and walk about even while doing something. Eating, reading, whatever it may be.

In combat, Rihne's magic focuses heavily on illusions and control of the battlefield. She generally abhors violence and actively avoids harming others whenever possible, but this doesn't extend to things that are not alive, such as undead.
