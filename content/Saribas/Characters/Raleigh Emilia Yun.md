## Backstory

## Appearance

Emilia is somewhat tall, standing above average height for a Sariban, likely thanks to her father's Weigriffian blood. Her blonde hair is kept short in the back but longer along the sides to frame her face with her bangs. Along with her height, the only other trait she pulled from her father is her brown eyes. Her olive toned skin keeps her almost out of place on the mainland of Auros, if she ventures outside of Saribas Town.

Her outfit is customized by her own hand and is an amalgamation of more traditional Sariban mystic garb and Weigriffian dancer garb. She wear a cloak over her clothing with a decorative brooch and a hood to help keep dirt off of her and her clothes.

Aside from a magical bag she keeps all her belongings in, she also carries two dancing fans she uses in her performances. They have semi-concealed bladed edges she can use to cut things.

## Personality


---




Mother: Reshia. Reshia's parents are Fayzn and Nyame. 
Father: Dann Manchester. Hails from Weigriff. Worked in the Weigriffian Military.
Don't have any other siblings.

The twins are their own best friends. It is hard to have a significant other when your sister must be so close all the time.

Grew up in Saribas Town, a district in Raleigh, Weigriff that is home to a growing number of Saribans. The family business focuses on history and features a small museum filled with sariban relics and various historical objects. With the growing district, our work included helping others with odd jobs and the like. 

While Nyame trained Emilia in the art of Mysticism, Emilia was more focused on the art form over the religion and science that came with. She ended up modifying some of the techniques used, merging them with a popular form of dancing in Auros that focuses on the use of fans. Rihne, Nyame's best friend and godmother to Emilia and Isobel, taught her medicine and led the charge for a time on researching the Twins' condition, though a resolution was never found. 

Emilia uses her own form of mystic dancing to enhance her allies, provide succor, and debilitate enemies. She is also schooled in medicine.

Emilia and her sister's social status is unusual. They did not attend their Assignment Ceremony due to being kidnapped at the time, and upon returning their unual circumstances necessitated other things beyond tradition. As time passed, the sisters became more and more focused on their circumstances.
  
Emilia has some reverence for Fayzn and Rihne; they were raised on their stories, in a land they helped save, in a community that would not exist if not for their choices and actions. So much of our lives and upbringing revolves around their deeds and adventures. Also Fayzn is literally our grandfather, and I imagine Rihne held a role of significant importance in our family, even if she was not frequently present, due to her close life-long relationship with both of our grandparents.

Also we love Jeffrey the most.

Emilia showed low interest in the Sariban faith taught by her mother and Rihne, but did not shun the teachings overall. Being raised in a foreign land, and then kidnapped as a child, impacted her focus on Sariban culture and traditions.

Does your character have any personal enemies?

Emilia wants to find the people that kidnapped her and her sister, but is apprehensive about it because she is unsure how she would react and handle the situation. She believes in justice, but is uncertain how to apply it.
  

Emilia is good natured and seeks to help others, but will also place a higher priority on Isobel, leading to some conflicts with others. She has skirted rules in the past.

Emilia seeks knowledge on the twins’ magical bond and its nature. She wants to be able to gain and master control over it to use it to their advantage, instead of having to deal with the downsides. Some of this studying has fallen to the side as the pair adapted to their situation and continued to grow up and live their lives.

Emilia largely does not care about the Assignment Ceremony any more. While it might be nice to have her name not mean child anymore, she is also gotten quite comfortable just being Emilia. Her mother was most insistent in going through with it in the past, but has long since given up as well.
  
Emilia has a disorder that makes it very difficult to deal with uncleanliness. The very first magic she mastered was magic that assists in cleaning things including herself. She will almost always refuse to do anything that would get her dirty, and annoys people with her insessant cleaning. She also had Rihne help her perform a ritual to enchant a broom that can always follow her around and assist with cleaning.  

Emilia has a Weigriffian accent, is very friendly and talkative, but easily flows into weirdly specific topics that can sometimes be uninteresting or uncomfortable to others.
