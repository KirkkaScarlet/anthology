>> ## Backstory
>> Born in Raleigh's Sariban Town. Aside from a brief stint where she and her twin sister were kidnapped, the two spent most of their life learning to blend their parent's different cultures together. While Emilia learned Sariban traditional dancing from their mother Reshia, Isobel took up fighting after their father Dann. 
>> 
>> Emilia went on to study both magic and medicine while on their quest to find a solution to the twins' problem.
>> ## Appearance
>> Emilia is somewhat tall, standing above average height for a Sariban. Her blonde hair is kept short in the back but longer along the sides to frame her face with her bangs. Aside from her brown eyes, she kept most of her sariban mother's traits.
>> 
>> Her typical outfit a mixture of traditional Sariban mystic garb and Weigriffian dancer garb. A cloak protects her from the harsh reality of dirt and grime. 
>> 
>> Aside from a magical bag she keeps all her belongings in, she also carries two bladed dancing fans she uses in her performances.
>> ## Personality
>> Emilia is friendly and quite sociable. Her demeanor speaks to her education about magic and medicine, and its obvious how much she likes dancing.
>> Her obsession with cleanliness is sometimes frustrating to deal with.
