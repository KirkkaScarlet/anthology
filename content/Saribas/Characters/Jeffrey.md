
## Backstory

Jeffrey’s early life was a strange one. First, there was nothingness. Then, a blinding light followed by dancing images all around him. It was incredible, albeit a little disorienting. Still, Jeffrey remained stalwart and steadfast as he readied himself for the coming unknown. 

Before long, the lights all faded, and Jeffrey looked around in the real world for the first time. The first thing he noticed was the human woman standing before him. He knew her already. Rihne was her name. How did he know her though? He’d never seen her before now. He pondered this using his Intellect Score of 1. He instantly forgot what he was thinking about however when he heard a voice. It was not in a language he knew, but he instantly understood. He was being greeted by the human woman in front of him. He nodded, then carefully climbed up her outstretched arm and stopped on her shoulder. Jeffrey was practically made of spikes and he knew and took great care to navigate the path carefully, as there is no worse offense in the universe than harming the divine god that brought him into existence. Yep. Divine. God. Jeffrey knows.

The next days and weeks were relatively peaceful. Jeffrey learned much about his new world and his god’s life. Her lifespan compared to his was unfathomably long, and she possessed infinite knowledge and unbelievable magical power. And there were others like her all over the land. It was like he’d been brought to the land of the gods. He had to be careful here, for upsetting any one of these beings could spell a horrible doom for him. He must remain on their good side. And so he did so with great care, ensuring that Rihne was always satisfied with his work on whatever tasks she assigned him. From standing on her shoulder to standing on the shoulders of other gods, to channeling Rihne’s magic through him and into another god. That experience was the most terrifying event in Jeffrey’s life. But it went well, overall. He lived, and so did the other god. A most auspicious moment.

One thing Jeffrey noticed was that all of the gods required rest for a significant part of the day. That alone wasn’t too shocking, and it certainly seemed like most of them enjoyed leaving this world for several hours per light cycle. The confusing part was that all of the gods required huge amounts of sustenance. It made sense that they required a lot of energy, but to think that gods had limits on their powers! He couldn’t believe it.

Several long years had passed. They’d been mostly peaceful. Jeffrey had adapted to his life as a follower of the Rihne. He made friends with several other followers, but they all always wondered why each god only had one follower. They dared not try to broach the subject however, as they never know how the gods would react to such disrespect to their way of existence.

Jeffrey has seen many things. Tall ones, short ones. But he had never seen this. A god so tall that it would be impossible to even consider comparing their height to Jeffrey. But, Jeffrey knew that gods generally did not mind when you climbed them. And if they did, Rihne was always nearby to guide her follower to safety. But Jeffrey had a wish. A wish to see the world. And he knew that, by scaling the tallest of tall ones, he could see it all from great heights. And so he approached it cautiously. The bottom parts of gods sometimes moved unpredictably, so it could be dangerous to move in without their knowledge. But he had to know. He had to see. He pushed forward. 

Success! The expedition had begun. Jeffrey slowly made it way toward the top of the god of nearly infinite tallness. He wondered if it was even possible to reach the top, but again, he had to know. 

Soon, he reached the top. Well, the shoulder. Jeffrey knew that climbing into the hair of a god was a deeply offensive act due to the spines covering his body. It was easy to become trapped, and panic could set in, causing unrest to all in the area due to extremely high pitched screams. But the shoulder. It was so high. He looked down upon his goddess for the first time. She was so small now. It almost made him question his existence compared to that of the gods. But he knew better. He had seen too much. He took in his surroundings. It was breathtaking. He could see for distances he did not even know existed.

After recovering from his epic journey, he discussed options with Rihne. He appreciated Rihne's shoulder, but from his current location, he could see everything. He desired to stay there. To his surprise, Rihne agreed. She explained that she can see what he can see--a typical godlike power--and that the entity he was resting on, who she referred to as Jomrik, was a powerful god and she would be able to aid him more easily in battle with Jeffrey at his side. Jeffrey was ecstatic to hear this and has spent the majority of this new adventure atop the great mountain of Jomrik.

## Appearance

Jeffrey is a lizard, first and foremost. He appears to be based on a type of lizard native to the Saribas Isles. They boast spines on almost all of their body, save the belly, making him very unappetizing to would-be predators. He also possesses scales that blend in with sandy and rocky terrain well, making it hard to spot him when he is unmoving on these terrains. 

Jeffrey has very little in the way of attire. He is mostly naked in the strictest sense of the word, though both Jeffrey and his gods have no issue with that, as they tend to consider his scales and skin itself as suitable attire for public settings.

Jeffrey's weapon is his body. Once, while locked in an epic struggle with his rival over the only sunbathed spot in Rihne's house, he bit his foe. Not too hard, but he desired the spot. Jeffrey doesn't like to talk about the events that took place afterward, save that he refers to it as the Day of Darkness.

## Personality

Upon first look, Jeffrey appears to have the personality and intelligence of a lizard.

Which is completely true.

Deep down, however, Jeffrey has a friendly and playful personality and has come to enjoy spending time with the gods and goddesses that invited him to journey through their universe. He cannot smile, but if he could, he would always be smiling. Except on the Day of Darkness.
