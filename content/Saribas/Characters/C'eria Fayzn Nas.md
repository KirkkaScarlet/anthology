
## Backstory

Fayzn hails from the Saribas Isles, a small collection of islands a few hundred miles to the south of the Stoneheart Mountains of Auros. The islands are home to a group of Dan'Illayan descended humans numbering in the thousands. They have spent some time being isolated from the rest of the world, as not many have reason to visit except a small collection of merchants to trade.

Tradition and religion are everything to the people of this island. Everyone on the island is taught these traditions at a young age alongside religious teaching. Ceremony is a very big deal for the people here. 

Saribas follows a very tribal design, with those gifted with savantism typically in leadership positions. Regardless of their status, savants are often accompanied by a warrior bodyguard at all times, all of whom belong to a tribe dedicated to this role of bodyguard, the Nās tribe. Fayzn has been a member of this tribe since reaching the Age of Assignment, like all Saribans. He has trained as a guardian of others for most of his life and specializes in the defense of others and the debilitation of enemies. 

Fayzn's life has been very simple. He was assigned as the second guard to the young C'xair Vyamme Ka'nan, a lazy good for nothing of a savant, as far as Fayzn was concerned. Vyamme spent most of her free time playing around with magic and disregarding her studies with the Kirye tribe, which often led to Fayzn having plenty of free time himself, which is how he met and befriended Rihne and Nyame, Vyamme's tutors. Rihne is an expert in Life magic while Nyame is an expert in Chaos magic. 

One day, while tracking Vyamme through the wilderness outside of T'koas after she used flying magic to get away from both him and his now companion Nyame, he was summoned by C'eria Tamir Ka'nan, the current leader of Saribas, for a critical mission. Tamir figured Fayzn's skill was being wasted trying to get a handle on Vyamme, and left Vyamme's other guards in charge of her. 

Fayzn was picked to join the second Auros expedition alongside Rihne, Nyame, and several other skilled guards. The first expedition set out just over six months before in search of information regarding The Plague, a mysterious chaos event that overtook the major land of Auros to the north of the Saribas Isles. The first group never returned, however, so a second team was being sent, tasked with finding them and bringing them back home. 

Upon arriving, it only took the group a few weeks to discover the fate of their previous group. An unexpected attack on the city from necromancers led to their deaths. Whether they were the target of this attack or not remained a mystery, but there were only about 35 deaths in total. All twelve saribans, and then several city guards.

Once this was discovered, the group spent a few days searching for additional information, before deciding to return. All except Rihne, that is. She was convinced that there was more going on than they were being led to believe and wanted to stay. The others were not on board with this idea, and all of them left except Fayzn, who stayed behind with Rihne as her guard. After a few days, Rihne and Fayzn joined with a caravan on the way out to Ashford, a city further inland. However, while on the road, just after dusk, the caravan was attacked. Fayzn and several other caravan guards and fighters jumped into the fray to protect the others. The battle turned in their favor thanks to a powerful mage that was along for the ride, but when Fayzn turned back to the caravan, Rihne's wagon was nowhere to be found. Someone claimed they saw that it had taken off toward Ashford against orders to circle the wagons for defense. Fayzn immediately began making his way there while the caravan collected it's wounded and dead, but after several minutes of traveling, he felt a strange feeling in the distance. He readied his weapon... but his memory of what happened, and everything beyond, won't come to him. He cannot recall any details beyond that battle at the caravan.

The next thing Fayzn was able to recall was waking up in the middle of the Stoneheart Mountains surrounded by Rihne and about eighteen others. Once they all exchanged information, it was learned that Fayzn had been under some form of mind control for about one season, basically since the Ashford event. He still is unable to recall any details between those two days, but there are constantly thoughts trapped in the back of his mind that push him forward, but he cannot explain his exact reasonings due to memory loss.

## Appearance

Fayzn is the tallest person in Saribas, at a height of 6'4". He has a young face, being only 22 years old. His short, messy black hair hangs in front of his eyes at times, though he claims he has no issues with vision. His skin is a dark olive color thanks to his spending most of his days outside for his entire life.

Members of the Nās tribe wear a ceremonial garb over any armor they choose to wear, which is a long, dark blue cardigan that covers his shoulders but is otherwise sleeveless. His armor is mostly underneath, comprised of chainmail with leader shoulder pads and straps. 

His weapon is also traditional for members of the Nās tribe, a polearm not unlike a Hsu Guandao. It has unique runes written on it and is enchanted as a defensive weapon. Additionally, he carries a spear and often a short bow, though his skill with the latter is underwhelming.

## Personality

Fayzn is a sarcastic, nonchalant, and loyal and straight-forward person. He playfully bullies people sometimes, and sometimes doesn't see the effect he can have on others. If he ever truly offends people though he is always quick to apologize, though that doesn't necessarily mean he will stop. 

In combat, Fayzn switches gears and lets his intense training guide him, largely devoid of emotion. He is always first to step in front of others to protect them, utilizing his magic to debilitate enemies and keep his allies protected. He is truly dedicated to his role as protector of others, even willing to throw away everything in sacrifice for others.
