- What happened to the Dan’Illayan people?
    

- In the year 12271 of the easterner’s calendar, our world was set upon by unmanned alien spacecraft that extracted numerous types and seemingly infinite amounts of natural resources. These crafts were only a few times larger than a human, so it was assumed some incredibly powerful magic technology was being used to send these resources directly to another location across deep space. 
    

  

- Once the Dan’Illayan’s were able to overcome the defenses of these alien drones, many were captured and studied for reverse engineering. From the components of these drones, Dan’Illayan engineers were able to create powerful energy generators that could help solve an impending energy crisis. However, they started experimentation with very small scale models out of safety concerns whilst they were perfecting the designs. These small models, while quite powerful, needed to be scaled up significantly to provide sufficient power on a national scale for centuries to come.
    

  

- On the 70th day of Winter in the 12293rd year, an unforeseen issue arose during one of the upscaling experiments due to a substantial change in expected behavior once passing a certain energy generation threshold. A chain reaction caused the generator to release a very large amount of energy, leveling much of western Qin’Lon and discharging incredibly lethal amounts of neutron radiation to the entire region. Little is known about what came after. Inhabitants of the library were protected from the radiation but were not able to leave. The last surviving library inhabitant passed away on or shortly after the 88th day of Autumn, 12294. 
    

  

- Evacuation efforts are assumed to have taken place but results are mostly unknown. It is assumed though uncertain that the inhabitants of current day Dan’Illaya who live by Lake Dan’Illay are descendants of survivors who successfully evacuated. It is also assumed, though uncertain, that the creatures reported to be roaming the area are descendants of survivors whose genes were radically altered each generation by extreme radiation. First contact after the event was made in 14207 by the former of the two surviving groups previously mentioned.

- What is the origin of magic?
    

- Magic is an intrinsic property of physics believed to have been present since the beginning of the universe. Due to its incredibly complex nature, it is uncertain if the physics of magic have changed over time into the rules we understand it to have today, but scientists are fairly confident it has always existed.
    

- How does magic work? (Chaos, Arcane, Life)
    

- Like physical matter, magic is comprised of fundamental components called mana, of which the physical counterpart would be atoms. These components can be arranged in up to three major variants, each of which has 100s of sub-variants which all interact with magical and/or physical matter in different ways. The physical counterpart to this would be elements, atoms whose properties are determined by the number of each subatomic particle they contain. After significant study and experimentation, there is no evidence to show that mana has any “subatomic particles” analogous to physical matter. Each of these variants and sub-variants are simply different ways in which a single “block” of mana can arrange itself.
    

  

- Through countless years of evolution and natural selection, biological creatures evolved ways to interact with magical matter directly. 
    

- Researches atomic theory to compare to knowledge of mana.
    

- Is magic limited in use? Can it theoretically be configured to do anything?
    

- Anything is an infinitely broad subject matter and nothing should ever be considered to have an infinite scope of functionality. That being said, current knowledge about mana estimates upwards of 1016 possible combinations and interactions of mana “blocks”. A large number of these may not do anything meaningful but that still leaves room for a large number of possibilities for the functionality of magic.

- What causes Savantism? Can it be gained by someone that wasn’t born with it?
    

- Savantism is a genetic mutation in which the magic interfacing systems of the body experience abnormal growth and resource allocation during fetal and early development. This alternate resource allocation often stunts some of the physical growth and capabilities of the individual.
    

  

- Because savantism is defined as a genetic birth condition, inducing savantism like conditions in an individual after birth would technically not be savantism anymore, but hormone induced magic saturation (HIMS). HIMS is an illegal practice due to an extraordinarily high death rate in all documented cases. Undergoers of the practice typically experience loss of functionality in many vital organs. Survivors of HIMS usually suffer permanent organ damage of some type, but in extremely rare cases have made full recoveries with the magical power and control of a savant but none of the other physical symptoms common in savantism. Lighter and well controlled versions of HIMS can be performed legally by medical professionals for a much lesser gain in magical power, usually with minimal side effects.

- The differences between variant species is assumed to be minor, mostly consisting of what comfort form(s) the species settles into. For the centaur of this world, the comfort form is that of a humanoid upper body and a horse like lower body. Prior to habitation on Auros, they took on a form with a similar upper body, but the lower body consisted of 6 even sized legs symmetrically extending outward from the bottom of the torso like arachnid legs. According to the centaur, the default forms their race takes are inspired by local fauna.
    

- How can I permanently become a Centaur?

But after completing biological analysis, there is a substantial bottle neck in obtaining such mastery due to the savantism induced frailty of your human DNA. Unlike most other forms of magic, shapeshifting can take quite a physical toll as well on the body of those who are not fit for it, either through practice or through genetics and biological design.

- How can I improve my communication skills with the spirits I see? (I accept that I see dead people, I want to be able to talk to them.)
    

- Though it is far more likely that you may be experiencing hallucinatory symptoms of schizophrenia, there are some documented cases in history of humans with manasight, the ability to perceive non-physical objects. Historical cases show a wide variety of objects perceived between individuals, including but not limited to: spirits, magical disturbances, the mana-field counterparts of spell casting, and sometimes even the mana-field itself.
    

  

- In cases where subjects saw spirits, there was inconsistency in when spirits would appear, and often involved additional factors such as seeing them through camera lenses, mirrors, or in particular lighting setups. This indicates there may be some psychological factor or trigger to this sight as well, as no cases ever demonstrated consistent mastery over the ability.
    

  

- Additionally, in cases where subjects saw spirits, their ability to directly interact was also inconsistent. Sometimes they would be able to speak with the spirits, but the spirits often uttered nonsense and could not hold any sort of regular conversation. It seemed as if the spirits were simply mana echoes of the former person, with bits of their programming showing through occasionally, but lacking any evidence of sentience. This is consistent with our knowledge and studies about spirits, which show that they do exist as sort of a magical remains of the former person. These magical remains are usually only generated in cases of extreme trauma at death or in cases where the former person was a powerful magic user.

Most documented cases also involved subjects with other psychological problems, so the magic may be a bit more complex than normal magic which is trained like any physical activity.
