# Nas

The Nas tribe is a subset of the Kas tribe. They are the elite soldiers who are usually tasked with protecting the Ka'nan and Kirye tribes when they venture far from the land. Members of the Nas tribe are often assigned a different tribe, usually Kas, at their Assignment Ceremony. Then, if they show promise as a fighter, they may request to join the Nas tribe, which the current "Ka'nan" approves or denies. If approved, they take on the new tribal title. Because this is a strictly volunteer based tribe, there are few members of the Nas tribe and even fewer who have received training but left their duties.

# Yun

The Yun tribal name is automatically given to all newborns. This tribe contains all children from birth until the age of assignment, which happens at eight year old.

# Ka'nan

Ka'nan is less of a tribe and more of a title. It replaces your tribal name when you found to have [[Savantism]]. Leaders are chosen from available Savants, typically around every 10 years. While the leader is revered highly, they work closely to lead with the Leadership tribe, After the leader retires, they maintain the Ka'nan title and are free to pursue their own desires. Due to how rare Savantism is, a leader sometimes has to retain the leadership position for longer.

# Aen

The Aen tribe is a smaller tribe that focuses on leading the Sariban people. They can be viewed as the governing body, working alongside the current Ka'nan to govern and enforce. The Kas tribe reports to members of the Aen tribe.

The Aen tribe is also responsible for assisting with raising of children, the Assignment Ceremony, and managing education. They direct leaders within other tribes on these matters, and have a dedicated system to help parents with their newborns, including such things like daycare.
