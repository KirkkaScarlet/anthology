> #### Traits
> **Hybrid:** Hybrid weapons combine two different melee weapons into a unique weapon that can function as either one. The hybrid weapon table lists the weapon statistics of each form. Switching between its different forms requires an Interact action, and you must be holding all of the weapon's parts. However, if your last action was a successful Strike against a foe with this weapon, you can make your next Strike this turn using the other form, switching forms as part of that Strike.
> Since a hybrid weapon is one weapon with multiple forms, both forms share any fundamental runes. If a form consists of more than one weapon, its runes are etched on one of them, chosen when the runes are etched. The other weapons in that form benefit from those runes only while the same creature wields all of them. You can put a property rune on a hybrid weapon as long as it is appropriate for either of the two forms, but if only one of the forms meets the property rune's requirements, the effects of the property rune only apply for that form. For instance, a *deflecting* twinfang only applies the *deflecting* property rune when you're using it as a double saber. Due to their complexity, hybrid weapons can't have another weapon, such as a shield boss or shield spikes, attached to them.
> A single Interact action draws or stows the whole weapon in either form as long as you have enough free hands for that form. If a form consists of more than one weapon, each is a separate weapon for effects such as Disarm and the twin trait.
> **Uncommon:** Something of uncommon rarity requires special training or comes from a particular culture or part of the world. Some character choices give access to uncommon options, and the GM can choose to allow access for anyone. Less is known about uncommon creatures than common creatures. They typically can't be summoned. The DC of [Recall Knowledge](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=26) checks related to these creature is increased by 2.
> **Finesse:** You can use your Dexterity modifier instead of your Strength modifier on attack rolls using this melee weapon. You still calculate damage using Strength.
> **Parry:** This weapon can be used defensively to block attacks. While wielding this weapon, if your proficiency with it is trained or better, you can spend a single action to position your weapon defensively, gaining a +1 circumstance bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.
> **Sweep:** This weapon makes wide swinging attacks. When you attack with this weapon, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your attack roll if you already attempted to attack a different target this turn using this weapon.
> **Agile:** The multiple attack penalty you take with this weapon on the second attack on your turn is –4 instead of –5, and –8 instead of –10 on the third and subsequent attacks in the turn.
> **Twin:** These weapons are used as a pair. When you attack with a twin weapon, you add a circumstance bonus to the damage roll equal to the weapon’s number of damage dice if you have previously attacked with a different weapon of the same type this turn. The weapons must be of the same type, but they don’t need to have the same runes.

> You are a hunter of mighty beasts. You've studied one of nature's deadliest predators─one capable of overcoming foes greater even than themselves: the viper. Swift and agile, with dual blades like fangs to bite or combine them into a single weapon to weave about the battlefield.
> You gain access to the twinfang weapon. For the purpose of proficiency, you treat the twinfang weapon as a martial weapon.
> You gain the Twinfang Bite feat.
> #### Traits
> **Archetype:** This feat belongs to an archetype.
> **Dedication:** Each archetype’s dedication feat represents your character’s dedicated effort learning a new set of abilities, making it impossible to split your focus and pursue another archetype at the same time. Once you take a dedication feat, you can’t select a different dedication feat until you complete your dedication by taking two other feats from your current archetype. You can’t retrain a dedication feat as long as you have any other feats from that archetype.

> ## Twinfang Bite ◆◆ <span class="level">Feat 2</span>
> <span class="trait">Archetype</span> <span class="trait">Flourish</span>
>
> ---
> **Source** Homebrew
> **Archetype** Viper
> **Prerequisites** Viper Dedication
> **Requirements** You are wielding a twinfang in any form.
> You strike with one form of your twinfang, quickly switching forms in one fluid motion. Choose one of the following options. Each Strike uses your current multiple attack penalty, and switching forms during this activity doesn't trigger reactions.
>  - **Double Saber** Strike a foe with the double saber, switch to dual fangs form, then Strike the same foe with one of the fangs. 
>  - **Dual Fangs** Strike a foe once with each fang, then switch the twinfang to its double saber form.
>
> If both attacks hit, combine their damage, and then add any other applicable effects from both weapons. You add any precision damage only once, to the attack of your choice. Combine the damage from both Strikes and apply resistances and weaknesses only once. This counts as two attacks when calculating your multiple attack penalty.
> #### Traits
> **Archetype:** This feat belongs to an archetype.
> **Flourish:** Actions with the flourish trait are special techniques that require too much exertion for you to perform frequently. You can use only one action with the flourish trait per round.
